Website structure

An audit reads the site the way a search engine reads it: page by page, checking the structure that decides what each page is about.

  • Page titles and descriptions, one clear subject per page
  • Headings in a logical order, with one main heading per page
  • Pages that compete with each other for the same search
  • Missing or misleading page details, listed with the page they belong to
Every finding names the page it applies to. Nothing is left as a general comment you cannot act on.

Google rankings

An audit records where the business shows up in ordinary Google results, for the searches its customers actually use, on the date of the check.

  • Which pages rank, and for which searches
  • Where the wrong page ranks for an important search
  • Searches where competitors rank and the business does not
  • Results labelled with the source and the date they were checked
Rankings move. An audit is a dated snapshot, not a promise that a position will hold.

Map results

For local businesses, the map result is often the first thing a customer sees. An audit compares the map listing with the website, line by line.

  • Business name, address details, hours and categories
  • Differences between the website and the map listing
  • Review counts, named by source and date, where a source provides them
  • Fixes in order of importance, starting with the ones customers see first
Where a source gives no rating or count, the audit says so. It does not fill the gap from another source.

AI answers

An audit asks the same questions a customer would ask, in fresh sessions, and records what Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Bing AI answer on that day.

  • The same set of questions asked of each AI tool, kept separate and dated
  • Whether the business is named, and in what position, in each answer
  • Which other businesses are named, where they are named
  • Prices or claims quoted by an AI tool, labelled as AI answers, not as business facts
AI answers change day to day. Results are a dated snapshot, and no AI mention is guaranteed.

What an audit does not do: it does not guarantee number one rankings or AI mentions, and it does not present a sample business as a client.
